1. Zero Data Collection & Privacy Commitment

Sanctuary is a private, encrypted 1-on-1 communication app designed for couples, best friends, and family. We do not log, store, track, or transfer any of your personal data, messages, or chat histories to any external central servers.

2. Encryption & Data Storage

All chat messages, emotion tags, and shared notes are sent and synchronized exclusively via Apple MultipeerConnectivity (P2P end-to-end encryption) or your personal Apple iCloud Private Database. Data remains strictly on your devices and your personal iCloud account.

3. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking

Sanctuary contains no third-party advertisements, tracking software, or analytics SDKs. Your personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party.

4. Data Deletion

You can clear your chat history anytime inside the app or delete the app completely, which permanently removes all locally stored data.

5. Contact Us & Support
